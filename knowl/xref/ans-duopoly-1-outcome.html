Task 1.9.9.d. The rational outcome.

Report \(q_1^*\text{,}\) \(q_2^*\text{,}\) the market price, and each firm’s profit.
🔗
Solution.
Again an upside-down parabola (the \(q_1^2\) coefficient is \(-\tfrac{1}{2}\)), so the critical point is the maximum: \(\dfrac{d}{dq_1}\bigl(68q_1 - \tfrac{1}{2}q_1^2\bigr) = 68 - q_1 = 0\text{,}\) so \(q_1^* = 68\text{.}\)
🔗
  • Quantities: \(q_1^* = 68\) and \(q_2^* = b(68) = 39\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • Market price: \(p^* = 150 - 68 - 39 = 43\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • Firm 1’s profit: \(\pi_1(q_1^*,q_2^*) = (43-9)(68) = 2312\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • Firm 2’s profit: \(\pi_2(q_1^*,q_2^*) = (43-4)(39) = 1521\text{.}\)
