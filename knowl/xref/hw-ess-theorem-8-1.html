(b)

Notice that \((a, a)\) and \((b, b)\) are Nash equilibria. Using the relevant results from Theorem 8.1 or Theorem 8.2, determine whether each of the population states \(a\) and \(b\) is evolutionarily stable.
🔗
Remark: To satisfy the conditions of Theorem 8.1, they must hold for every \(\tau \neq \sigma\text{.}\) To fail them, it is enough to find one \(\tau\) where a condition fails. Also, be careful to distinguish between a population state \(\sigma\) and a profile \((\sigma, \sigma)\) or \((\sigma, \tau)\text{.}\) For example, it does not make sense to say that \((\sigma, \sigma)\) is evolutionarily stable.
