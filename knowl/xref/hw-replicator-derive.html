Exercise 1. Deriving the Replicator Equation.

Consider the following symmetric game:
🔗

(a)

Let \(\sigma = p_1 a + p_2 b\) denote the population state. Derive the replicator system for this game (a system of two differential equations \(\dot{p}_1 = g(p_1, p_2)\) and \(\dot{p}_2 = h(p_1, p_2)\)).
🔗
🔗

(b)

Let \(p_1 = p\text{,}\) so that \(p_2 = 1 - p\text{.}\) Reduce the system from part (a) to a single differential equation \(\dot{p} = f(p)\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
You should expect \(f(p)\) to have both \(p\) and \(1 - p\) as factors.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
\(\dot{p} = p(1 - p)(4 - 5p)\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(c)

Find the equilibria of \(\dot{p} = f(p)\) in \([0, 1]\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

(d)

Without using your formula for \(f(p)\text{,}\) explain why we already know that \(p = 0\) and \(p = 1\) must be equilibria. Which theorem tells us this? Then explain which theorem tells us that the interior equilibrium (the one between 0 and 1) is where both strategies earn the same payoff against the population state at that equilibrium, and why.
