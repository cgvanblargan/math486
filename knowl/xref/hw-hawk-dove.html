Exercise 1. Hawk and Dove.

The Hawk-Dove game is a two-player game used to analyze animal behavior in territorial conflicts among animals of the same species. There are two strategies available to each player.
🔗
  • hawk (\(h\)): fight aggressively until you prevail or are injured.
    🔗
    🔗
  • dove (\(d\)): bluff by displaying, but retreat if the other player begins to fight.
    🔗
    🔗
We use two parameters, both positive real numbers.
🔗
  • \(v\text{:}\) the value of the territory or object being fought over.
    🔗
    🔗
  • \(w\text{:}\) the cost of getting injured in fighting.
    🔗
    🔗
