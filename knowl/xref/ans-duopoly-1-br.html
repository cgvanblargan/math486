Task 1.9.9.b. Firm 2’s best response.

Find \(b(q_1)\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
With \(q_1\) held fixed, \(\pi_2\) is a quadratic in \(q_2\) with \(q_2^2\) coefficient \(-1 \lt 0\text{,}\) so it is an upside-down parabola and its critical point is the global maximum. There is no boundary to check, since \(q_2\) ranges over all real numbers.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{\partial \pi_2}{\partial q_2} = 146 - q_1 - 2q_2 = 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad b(q_1) = 73 - \tfrac{1}{2}q_1. \end{equation*}
