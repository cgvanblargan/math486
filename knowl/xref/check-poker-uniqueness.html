Checkpoint 5.5.2. Only One Equilibrium in Poker.

Setup. In Section 5.4 we found the Nash equilibrium \((\sigma_1^*, \sigma_2^*)\text{,}\) where
\begin{align*} \sigma_1^* \amp = \tfrac{1}{5}\,bb + \tfrac{2}{5}\,bf + \tfrac{2}{5}\,f\\ \sigma_2^* \amp = \tfrac{8}{15}\,bb + \tfrac{2}{15}\,bf + \tfrac{1}{3}\,f \end{align*}
In both, all three strategies are active (every pure strategy gets positive probability). At the end of Section 5.4, the book also points out the following.
🔗
Fact from 5.4: In any Nash equilibrium of One-Card Two-Round Poker where Player 2 uses all three of his strategies, Player 1 must play \(\sigma_1^*\text{.}\)
